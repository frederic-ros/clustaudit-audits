The battery

Every audit runs on the same 250 datasets (battery paper-v2.2): the 75-dataset suite and six sweeps in which one factor of the generator varies while all the others stay fixed. Each dataset has 1000 points in 20 dimensions and 5 clusters; each level of a sweep is generated with five seeds.

Below, one dataset per level (seed 42), drawn from 300 of its points in a 2-D linear discriminant view fitted on the true clusters: the most separated linear view, so any overlap left in the picture is real. Colour: true cluster; grey: noise (points far outside the frame are drawn on its edge). D is the difficulty measured by clustaudit's profile. The files and their checksums are in the repository.
